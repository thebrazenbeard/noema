# Working intelligence criteria

Status: **BRAINSTORMING / PROVISIONAL**

Noema should be judged by behavior that depends on persistent internal state and learning from experience, not by conversational fluency.

Candidate criteria:

- **Learning rather than parroting:** novel experience changes later behavior and generalizes beyond the exact example.
- **Persistent, attributable memory:** experiences remain tied to the correct agent, time, context, and source and can be recalled later.
- **Causal understanding:** the system can distinguish useful causal hypotheses from mere correlation and can test them through action.
- **Prediction and surprise:** it forms expectations about future world states and treats meaningful prediction error as evidence.
- **Self-correction:** it can identify that its own model or strategy failed, determine what deserves revision, and change later behavior.
- **Other-agent modeling / empathy:** it models another agent's likely perceptions, knowledge, wants, needs, or intentions and adapts appropriately rather than merely emitting empathic language.
- **Endogenous wants and needs:** some priorities arise from maintained internal state, and learned drives can emerge through experience.
- **Consistency without rigidity:** the agent remains recognizably continuous while still revising beliefs, preferences, and strategies.
- **Planning:** it represents desired future states, compares alternatives, acts, and revises plans as the world changes.
- **Counterfactual reasoning:** it can reason about plausible alternatives to what actually occurred.
- **Information seeking / curiosity:** uncertainty can motivate targeted investigation when reducing uncertainty matters.
- **Transfer:** learned structure can be applied in meaningfully different situations.
- **Metacognition:** it can distinguish knowing, suspecting, remembering, inferring, uncertainty, and recognized error.
- **Self-development / self-actualization:** it can model aspects of its own current operation, identify meaningful capability gaps or conflicts, and deliberately change how it operates.
- **Initiative:** internally detected incompleteness, conflict, opportunity, or uncertainty can lead to appropriate investigation or action without an external prompt specifying that exact action.

## Important distinction

A persuasive answer is not itself evidence of these capacities. Tests should be constructed so that internal persistence, learning, prediction, correction, and transfer are necessary to succeed.
